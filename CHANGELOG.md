# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- Android and iOS: a vinyl Disc with a Vinyl color can carry a Finish, set under the Vinyl color: Solid (the default), Marble, Splatter, Split, Galaxy or Glitter. It shows on the Disc out of its sleeve and in the form; the Color band on covers stays flat in the Vinyl color. Every Disc has its own pattern. Existing Discs stay Solid. The Catalog does not prefill it.
- Android and iOS: Marble, Splatter, Split and Galaxy take a Second color, picked from the Vinyl colors plus Black. It starts as White (Black for Split and Galaxy) and can be changed.
- Android and iOS: six new Vinyl colors: Gold, Silver, Grey, Brown, Teal and Bone.
- Android and iOS: Glitter and Galaxy shimmer as the phone tilts, unless the system asks to reduce motion.
- Android and iOS: the back of a card shows the Finish and colors as text on the Speed line (for example "33 tr/min · Marbré · Rouge et noir"), and the cover's screen reader label includes them ("Vinyle couleur rouge marbré noir", "red marble vinyl with black").

### Changed
- Android and iOS: the Profile card is now a portrait card brushed in diagonal lines, and it shows the Collection stats: the Collection value (and how many Discs have no Paid price), the number of vinyls, CDs and colored vinyls, and the oldest year. The Collection value by Format below it is gone.

## v1.2.2 - 2026-11-21

### Added
- Android and iOS: the app is available in English and French. It follows the device Language by default (English when the device uses another one), and the user can pick a Language in the Profile view, which overrides the device Language. The Speed is shown as "33 tr/min" in French and "33 rpm" in English; "Wishlist" stays "Wishlist" in both.
- Android: a Language picked in the system Settings (per-app language) is picked up by the app, and the Profile view shows the Language the app is actually displayed in.
- iOS: the app name shown under the icon is translated in French.

### Changed
- Android and iOS: prices and dates follow the Language: "1 240,50 €" and "3 nov. 2024" in French, "€1,240.50" and "Nov 3, 2024" in English. The Seen price and Paid price fields use the Language's decimal separator ("12,50" in French, "12.50" in English).
- iOS: a few English wordings and formats now read exactly like Android.

## v1.2.1 - 2026-11-21

### Added
- Android: a vinyl Disc can carry a Speed (33 or 45), set with a 33 | 45 control under Vinyl color and shown on the Disc detail as "33 tr/min" or "45 tr/min". Existing Discs stay unset.
- iOS: a vinyl Disc can carry a Speed (33 or 45), set with a 33 | 45 control under Vinyl color (tap the selected value to clear it) and shown on the Disc detail as "33 tr/min" or "45 tr/min". Existing Discs stay unset; the Catalog does not prefill it.
- iOS: the app now exists on iOS, with the same features as Android: Collection and Wishlist, Disc detail with the flipping cover, Search (My discs and the Catalog, Discogs then MusicBrainz), barcode Scan, Seen price and Paid price, the Profile card and the Collection value by Format.

### Changed
- The round sticker on the cover of a colored vinyl Disc is now a flat Color band across the top-right corner, on Android and iOS.
- New app icon on Android and iOS.
