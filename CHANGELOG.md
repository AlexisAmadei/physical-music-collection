# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- Android: a vinyl Disc can carry a Speed (33 or 45), set with a 33 | 45 control under Vinyl color and shown on the Disc detail as "33 tr/min" or "45 tr/min". Existing Discs stay unset.
- iOS: a vinyl Disc can carry a Speed (33 or 45), set with a 33 | 45 control under Vinyl color (tap the selected value to clear it) and shown on the Disc detail as "33 tr/min" or "45 tr/min". Existing Discs stay unset; the Catalog does not prefill it.
- iOS: the app now exists on iOS, with the same features as Android: Collection and Wishlist, Disc detail with the flipping cover, Search (My discs and the Catalog, Discogs then MusicBrainz), barcode Scan, Seen price and Paid price, the Profile card and the Collection value by Format.

### Changed
- The round sticker on the cover of a colored vinyl Disc is now a flat Color band across the top-right corner, on Android and iOS.
- New app icon on Android and iOS.
